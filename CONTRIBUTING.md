# Contributing to Settlements

## Standards

Conventions live in `docs/standards/`, one document per concern, each written to be read on its own. Read the one
matching what you are about to touch **before you start**; `docs/standards/README.md` indexes them and explains how
rules are numbered, cited, and retired.

These set the bar, and work below it gets reworked regardless of who wrote it. Review comments cite rule ids rather than
restating the rule, so a bare `T5` in a review is a pointer to go read rather than shorthand for "I disagree".

## Licensing of contributions (inbound = outbound)

By submitting a contribution to this project, you agree that:

- **Code** contributions are licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
- **Asset** contributions — art, textures, sounds, models, and other creative files — are licensed under the Creative
  Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0) (see [LICENSE-ASSETS](LICENSE-ASSETS)).

You keep the copyright in your own contributions; you are licensing them to the project and its users under the terms
above — not signing them away.

Once a contribution is released under these licenses, that grant is **irrevocable** for copies already distributed: you
cannot later "un-license" work that has already shipped in a release. You are, of course, free to stop contributing at
any time, and you may ask for an asset of yours to be dropped from **future** releases (which the maintainers may honor
as a courtesy).

Contributors are recognized in the [AUTHORS](AUTHORS) file, which is curated by the maintainers. The Git commit history
is the authoritative record of authorship, so nothing is lost if you aren't listed there individually.

## Asset provenance (please read before submitting art or sound)

Only submit assets that **you personally created**, or that you have a clear, documented right to license under CC BY
4.0. Do **not** submit:

- assets found online without a compatible license,
- edits or derivatives of someone else's work without their permission,
- assets extracted from other games or mods.

If an asset is third-party (used with permission) or derived from Minecraft's own assets, it does **not** fall under the
project's CC BY 4.0 grant. Record it in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) instead, with its source, creator, and license.
